Customer Support Guidelines

Verified channels, security protocols, and procedures for accessing assistance and resolving disputes safely with Moniepoint.

!

Official Support Warning

Moniepoint agents will NEVER ask for your transaction PIN, login password, or OTP. Only use verified channels listed below to prevent fraud.

📞

Official Channels

  • In-App Chat: Utilize the instant live chat tool built directly into your mobile app dashboard.
  • Phone Support: Call official lines at +234 201 888 9990 or +234 201 330 7550.
  • Email & WhatsApp: Reach general support via support@moniepoint.com or verified WhatsApp at +234 908 843 0803.
🚨

Fraud & Security Desk

  • Fraud Reporting: Send urgent reports regarding unauthorized transfers or suspicious activities to frauddesk@moniepoint.com.
  • Emergency Block: Use the in-app freeze or card block toggle instantly if your account or device is compromised.
  • Social Verification: Ensure social media handles (@moniepointng on X and Instagram) feature official verification badges.
📝

Dispute Resolution

  • Required Details: Always provide your full name, account number, transaction reference ID, and date when lodging complaints.
  • Resolution Window: Allow standard processing times for investigations on failed or delayed transactions.
  • Branch Assistance: Visit authorized Moniepoint service centers or physical hubs across Nigeria for complex issues.
