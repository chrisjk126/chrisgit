Moniepoint Personal Banking Guidelines

Personal Usage Guidelines

Essential standards and security best practices to help you manage your Moniepoint personal account safely and effectively.

!

Critical Security Warning

Moniepoint staff will NEVER call, message, or email you requesting your 4-digit PIN, account password, or OTP. Never share credentials under any circumstance.

🔐

Account Setup & Security

  • Identity Alignment: Ensure your BVN and NIN match your official identification documents during registration.
  • Biometrics: Enable Fingerprint or Face ID authentication for secure, multi-factor login access.
  • Device Access: Limit application login strictly to your primary mobile phone.
⚡

Transfers & Limits

  • Tier Upgrades: Submit required KYC documentation to upgrade from Tier 1 to Tier 3 for increased transfer limits.
  • Receipt Check: Always verify inward transaction alerts inside the app inbox rather than relying solely on SMS notifications.
  • Dispensations: Check transaction history status before retrying pending or delayed transfers.
💳

Card Safety & Support

  • Instant Freeze: Use the in-app card toggle to block your debit card immediately if misplaced or stolen.
  • ATM Usage: Ensure no unauthorized individual views your transaction PIN at POS terminals or ATMs.
  • Official Support: Report disputes directly through the in-app help center or verified official email channels.
