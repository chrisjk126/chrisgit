Moniepoint Business Banking Guidelines

Business Usage Guidelines

Operational standards, compliance rules, and best practices for managing your Moniepoint business account and POS network safely.

!

Compliance & Account Notice

Ensure your business inflows match your registered commercial profile. Unverified or suspicious transactions can trigger automated compliance restrictions (PND).

📋

Verification & Compliance

  • CAC & TIN Details: Upload valid corporate registration documents and Tax Identification Numbers matching your enterprise profile.
  • KYC Alignment: Ensure directors' and business owners' BVN and NIN information correspond correctly across all records.
  • Permits: Provide relevant industry operating licenses or SCUML certificates where legally mandated.
⚡

Transactions & POS Security

  • Terminal Security: Protect your physical POS terminals and prevent unauthorized third-party handling or access.
  • Transparent Inflows: Keep proper records, receipts, and invoices for business transactions to justify sudden volume spikes.
  • Restricted Activities: Strictly refrain from utilizing business accounts for prohibited operations such as cryptocurrency trading.
🤝

Management & Support

  • Daily Reconciliation: Use the built-in Moniepoint dashboard tools to reconcile sales, payouts, and ledger entries.
  • Relationship Officers: Coordinate directly with your assigned Branch Relationship Manager (BRM) for terminal or account assistance.
  • Official Channels: Direct all formal complaints and dispute reports through the verified merchant app help desk.
