Moniepoint Privacy Policy & Guidelines

Data Privacy & Policy Guidelines

Overview of how Moniepoint collects, utilizes, and protects your personal and financial information under regulatory compliance frameworks.

i

Commitment to Data Protection

Moniepoint processes all personal user information in alignment with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) and applicable regulatory standards to safeguard your privacy.

📊

Data Collection Scope

  • Personal Details: Collection of names, phone numbers, email addresses, photos, and official identification numbers (BVN, NIN).
  • Financial Info: Transaction logs, bank accounts, and merchant settlement details.
  • Technical Identifiers: Tracking IP addresses, MAC addresses, device IMEIs, and SIM info for security verification.
⚙️

Processing & Usage

  • Verification & KYC: Utilizing data to verify identity, complete account onboarding, and adhere to anti-money laundering (AML) laws.
  • AI & Automation: Employing automated models and AI for real-time fraud monitoring, risk scoring, and credit evaluations.
  • Legal Obligation: Sharing data with regulatory or law enforcement bodies strictly when required by law or formal directive.
🔒

User Rights & Security

  • Data Access & Rectification: Rights to request access to personal information held or correct incomplete records.
  • Data Erasure: Options to request deletion of application profiles or data limits via the official Data Protection Officer.
  • Account Security: Users carry primary responsibility for guarding login credentials and transaction keys.
